        How the numbers work↑ Top

        Costs

        • Per stop: getting there, stay (per-night rate × nights), activities and local transport, and food. Family of 4 (2 adults, kids 7 and 10). AUD; €1 = $1.62.
        • R is a researched price (Google Flights, Booking.com, vr.fi or the operator, 20–22 Oct 2026); E is my estimate. Food at the cooking stops is an E built from researched supermarket prices. "Option" items (glass igloo, snowmobile, guided aurora) count only in the high case.
        • Not included: the booked long-haul flights, insurance, shopping, alcohol.

        Usable hours

        • Daylight: Paris 08:35–16:55, Iceland 11:15–15:30, Rovaniemi about 11:00–13:25 (safaris run in twilight and dark to about 15:00 and beyond), Helsinki 09:25–15:10, Stockholm 08:40–14:50.
        • Arrival afternoon = sunset − arrival − 0.5 h. Full day = start to sunset minus lunch. Indoor and evening items use a 2.5 to 3 h evening window.
        • Verdicts: Worth it, Marginal (late arrival, early start or leaning on the evening), Night on board (train), Pointless as a night.

        Removing and editing

        • Removing a stop merges its travel into the next leg (sum of both, an estimate) and frees its nights. The flights fix the window, so spare nights are shown, not hidden.
