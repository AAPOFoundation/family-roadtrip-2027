    Paris: protest exposure ranking

    Based on the 17 Oct 2026 events and the banned zones that day (Champs-Élysées, Concorde, Châtelet–Les Halles, the Palais de Justice area). Marches usually run République, Bastille, Nation, and the damage was around République, Boulevard de Magenta, Sébastopol, Madeleine and Faubourg-Saint-Honoré. Ranked highest first; the "Planned" column follows the current plan. This is my judgement from news reports, not an official map. Check the news and préfecture de police notices each morning. Weekdays are not automatically safe: 13 Oct (a Tuesday) also had serious incidents.

      How the numbers work

      Costs

      • Per stop: getting there, stay (per-night estimate × nights), activities and local transport, and food. Family of 4 (2 adults, 2 kids ~7–9). AUD at EUR 1 = AUD 1.62.
      • R means a researched published price (Annecy hotels, 2 Oct 2026 search); E is my estimate, not a quote. Low, likely and high are ranges.
      • Not included: the booked long-haul flights (on points), insurance, shopping, alcohol.

      Usable hours

      • Daylight: Paris and Annecy 08:15–17:00; Reykjavík 11:15–15:30 in late December. Arrival afternoon = sunset − arrival − 0.5 h. Full day = start to sunset minus lunch.
      • Indoor and evening items (Perlan, pools, aurora, Christmas lights) use a 2.5 to 3 h evening window each night.
      • Verdicts: Worth it (plan fits), Marginal (late arrival, early start or reliance on the evening), Pointless as a night (more than half the plan cannot happen).

      Removing and risk

      • Removing a stop merges its travel into the next leg (sum of both, an estimate) and frees its nights. The flights fix the window, so spare nights are shown, not hidden.
      • Risk badges are per stop: strike, unrest, security and other. The Annecy badge turns red if the train falls inside the strike notice.
