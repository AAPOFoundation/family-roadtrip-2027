Experiences, tiered (S / A / B)

Dec 2026 · 2 adults + kids aged 7 and 10 · every candidate stop. Tiers are weighted by novelty: can you do it anywhere else, and especially in Australia? Priorities: animals (huskies, reindeer, whales), snow adventure, northern lights, unique stays, Christmas magic and hot lagoons. Cities, food and museums rank high only when they're one of a kind. S = one of a kind and trip-defining · A = unique and worth paying for if you're there · B = nice but available elsewhere, or only if convenient. Costs are for the whole family in AUD (child fares use the kids' ages in Dec 2026: 7 and 10). Costs marked est. are estimates; the rest are official prices.

Add-on: 2 nights at Höfn / Jökulsárlón (optional)

Not needed for an ice cave any more, since Katla FastTrack from Vík takes 6+. This is only worth it for the Crystal cave, the Jökulsárlón iceberg lagoon and Diamond Beach (icebergs on black sand).

  • Driving and daylight: Selfoss → Jökulsárlón is ~320 km, 4–4.5 h in winter (Vík → Jökulsárlón ~190 km, ~2.5–3 h). Mid-December light is about 11:15–15:30 plus ~1 h of twilight each side, so each way uses one day's light. Route 1 can close in storms, and the Öræfi stretch gets wind warnings. Check road.is and vedur.is each morning, and keep a spare day either side.
  • A, C1, C2 (11–12 Iceland nights): swap 2 Selfoss nights (e.g. 20–22 Dec, before the Christmas shutdown) for Höfn / Hof / Skaftafell. Iceland nights and the Christmas cabin stay the same. Cost about +$970 (range $900–1,300): Crystal cave $800, extra fuel ~$130, and east lodging at about $480 a night against Selfoss at ~$460 (estimate, not live-checked). You lose 2 relaxed days to driving and gain Jökulsárlón, Diamond Beach, Skaftafell and the cave.
  • B (7 nights): only works as Reykjavík 2 · Vík 2 · Jökulsárlón 2 · near KEF 1, which drops the Selfoss/Golden Circle base and adds ~9 h of winter driving. About +$1,100. Not recommended.
  • D (5 nights): doesn't fit without cutting the Golden Circle. Do Katla from Vík instead.
