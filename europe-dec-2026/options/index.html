Accor points and Accor+

Points value (verified on all.accor.com): 2,000 points = €40, used in 2,000-point steps (1,000 = €20 minimum online). 40,000 points ≈ €800 ≈ $1,296. Usable on room and on-site spend at participating Accor hotels; book a flexible rate so points come back if you cancel. Prepaid non-refundable rates need part card payment.

Where to use them: the value per point is fixed, so use them where you'd stay at Accor anyway and on flexible rates: Paris 10–12 (e.g. Novotel Paris Centre Tour Eiffel family room, or an Adagio aparthotel with kitchen) and the CDG night 30–31 (Novotel or Pullman CDG). Those three nights are worth roughly $900–1,000 (E), so about 28,000–30,000 points; put the rest on Accor breakfasts/dinners during those stays, or on Novotel Prague (option 3) or a Zürich Novotel night (option 7). The Tatras, Norway, Kiruna and Saariselkä bases have no Accor hotels.

Accor+ Explorer in Europe: per the T&Cs updated 19 May 2026, the member rate is 15% off the best available non-promotional public rate, worldwide. The Stay Plus free-night certificate and the dining discount apply only in the participating countries (Asia-Pacific and Middle East, including Qatar), not in Europe. Platinum gets no free breakfast in Europe.

Not verified: live Accor+ prices. all.accor.com didn't return results to an unauthenticated search, and Booking.com showed the Paris, CDG and Budapest Accor hotels as unavailable for 2 adults + 2 children. Check while signed in.

The brightest contrast with Iceland: twice the daylight, sunny 3,000 m views and the things Iceland doesn't have (night toboggans, sleighs, Alpine animals, crystal worlds, Silent Night) from one apartment with no internal flights. Single base, daytime direct flight, train home. About $4.3k more than Slovakia; $1.3k under Switzerland.

Day by day

  • Sat 19 Dec: Icelandair KEF 07:20 → Munich 12:05; hire car; ~2 h to Mayrhofen in daylight; supermarket
  • Sun 20: Hintertux glacier lifts to 3,250 m; Nature's Ice Palace tour 10:30 (beat the holiday queues)
  • Mon 21: Innsbruck: Alpenzoo, Nordkette cable car to 2,256 m, Old Town Christmas market (ends 23 Dec)
  • Tue 22: Swarovski Crystal Worlds (Wattens); Erlebnistherme Zillertal slides in Fügen

  • Sat 26–Sun 27: Zugspitze from Ehrwald (~2 h) on the clearest day; snowshoe walk; second night toboggan (Sat)
  • Mon 28: Krimml frozen waterfalls guided winter walk 11:00 (via Gerlos Pass, 50 min)
